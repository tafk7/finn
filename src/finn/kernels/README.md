# `finn.kernels`

Physical kernels: each binds one FinnLib RTL module (or an HLS top FINN writes over a
FinnLib HLS component) or places kernel children, never both,
in a design space in `finn.core.space`, with the order it presents on each port.
The package never reads an ONNX graph; the KernelOps, which do, live in
`finn.custom_op.kernels`. Experimental: its design and authoring guide are maintained
outside this repository until the package is final.

| Module | Holds |
|---|---|
| `base.py`, `declaration.py`, `library.py`, `port.py`, `channels.py`, `adapters.py` | the one kernel base and what a kernel states (`KernelSpace`: its netlist, cycles, buffering and resources as views written once over its declarations and its members' answers, reached through `Members` over their exports; a kernel binding a module declares it, `LibraryModule`, with its parameters, its sources, checked to provide the module, and its data, and states its ports and resources, its module read back from its netlist by `library_module`; a kernel with children places them and presents their ports, `served`, and states no stream port of its own; its cycles its ports' beats or its members', its `clock_domain`), what a reader asks of any kernel's design point (`Kernel`, a `typing.Protocol` of the four answers, read out), a declaration of a kernel lowered into its members when its class is made (`declaration.py`: `KernelDeclaration`, its arguments read by name, what the answers read of it, where its pins and hold go in the module: `ModuleOrder`), the ports (`AxiStreamPort`, lowered to `<p>_element`, `<p>_stream`, `<p>_access` and `<p>_contract`, a `PortContract`; `native_stream`, a channel stage's on FinnLib's native pins, presenting a given sequence; `served`, a kernel with children's port, its child's; the views: pins, transport, beats, pace and contract as functions), the `Channel` (one kernel with children per edge, taking the contracts of the ports on it as facts: its plan, adapters, transport and source; its netlist its members' and its hops, its cycles, buffering and resources its members', none for a channel of wires) and the adapters planned between its sides |
| `dotp.py`, `matmul.py`, `thresholding.py`, `eltwise.py`, `fifo.py`, `memstream.py`, `input_generator.py`, `transpose.py`, `vpc.py`, `pool.py`, `pwpolyf.py` | the kernels (`pool.py`: the first HLS kernel, over `pooling_stream`; `pwpolyf.py`: GELU, SiLU, Sigmoid or Tanh by piecewise polynomials on DSP58, FLOAT32 and approximate) |
| `configure.py`, `policies.py`, `cut.py` | committing choices by key, and naming the open and the stored ones; each choice's policy (fixed, first valid in listed order or in a ranking view's, or required), stated by the compiler: a KernelOp for its kernel's choices, the cut for a channel's (`cut.py`: `CHANNEL_POLICIES`), read through one reader (`policy`) and applied once (`defaulted`): neither the engine nor a kernel states one |
| `network.py` | the network: kernels and channels by name, each its own space bound from plain facts (a kernel given bound already, by its owner's cache), wired by the contracts each member states for its ports (`Interface`) and what a kernel states for its parameter channel (`Fact`); its members' stored choices set as one change as each is bound (a value outside its domain dropped as stale, a rule they break refusing the bind); a change (`trial`) atomic over the members it reaches, a rule it breaks refusing it by name; the cut's policies where a change rebuilds a channel (`cut.channel_defaulted`; each member's policy owner, `owner`); its composed module (`artifacts/top.py`'s `compose`; its netlist merged by the base's `merged`, as a kernel merges its members'), each member under its node. The graph binds one from a selection of KernelOp nodes (`finn.custom_op.kernels.network`) |
| `explore.py`, `fifo_sizing.py` | the DSE seam (`Seam`) over a network, its keys `(owner, key)` pairs: its one change (`set`, one engine change, refused with each rule it breaks and the choices standing in its way), the open choices with their possible values (`choices`), its strategies (`Ranked`, `Hold`, `TargetThroughput`, `MaxThroughput`, `SizeFifos`, `Defaults`, the debug `DebugPlaceholder`), and a strategy's run (`reopen` its scope, search, `restore` what it reopened, a value that cannot be restored set by its policy); a channel's FIFO depth from both sides' beat patterns |
| `control.py`, `movers.py`, `utilization.py` | control buses (`ControlBus`, a kernel's bus, held idle or presented and exported as `CONTROL`; a network presents its members'), a boundary's mover (`IodmaMover`: what a shell offers at a free side, its contract and resources), and the kernels' cost model: a memory's primitives on the platform's fabric, its styles, the fitted counts (`utilization.py`, over `finn.platform.capabilities`' `Fabric` and `Resources`; `uram_constraint`, `uram_init_constraint`) |
| `values/` | the values kernels hold and ports admit: the semantics of datatypes, integer vectors and tensors, and threshold tables; the `Integer` port policy |
| `transport.py` | ready/valid and AXI-Stream transports (`ReadyValidTransport`, `AxisBeat`), and the contract of one side of a channel (`StreamContract`, with its `pace`) |
| `artifacts/` | module values (`Leaf`, a flat `Composed` netlist) and their ABI (`abi.py`, with a module's clocking pins, `Clocking`), the top (`top.py`: a netlist wrapped as a `Composed` with FINN's top conventions, `compose`), their sources (`contributions.py`: copied files, generated data, HLS build requests), their emission, an HLS request's staging (`hls.py`), the RTL checker (and `evaluate`, the constants a module derives at elaboration), the IP-XACT packaging Tcl (`ipxact.py`), a packaged module's `interface.json` (`interface.py`) and the canonical digest (`projection.py`) |

FinnLib is the `finnlib` resource (`src/finn/resources.toml`); set
`FINN_RESOURCES_FINNLIB` to work against a clone. Checks:
`bash scripts/check-kernels.sh`.
