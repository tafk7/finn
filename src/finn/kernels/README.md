# `finn.kernels`

Physical kernels: each binds one FinnLib RTL module (or an HLS top FINN writes over a
FinnLib HLS component) or places kernel children, never both,
in a design space in `finn.core.space`, with the order it presents on each port.
The package never reads an ONNX graph; the KernelOps, which do, live in
`finn.custom_op.kernels`. Experimental: its design and authoring guide are maintained
outside this repository until the package is final.

| Module | Holds |
|---|---|
| `base.py` | the one kernel base, `Kernel`; a reader types a design point `Kernel` and reads its four answers. A channel's FIFO answers its `frame_cycles` inapplicable, which its parent's `member_frame_cycles` leaves out. A kernel binding a module states its binding (`module_spec`, `parameters`, `module_sources`, `module_data`) and its ports; the base assembles its netlist from them, reading its other pins, control bus and ports serially. A kernel whose ports own their pacing states its `extents` and its one `schedule` (the base's is inapplicable) by `finn.dataflow.schedule`'s rules, through `bound_extents`, `bound_schedule` and `spanned`, which give dataflow's refusals as the kernel's (`kernel-extents`, `kernel-schedule`); its folding's domains read an extent through `extent_of`. A kernel binding a module states no cycles, nor the base's private rate law behind them (`kernel-cycles-restated`), and a kernel with none states no interval (`kernel-interval-composite`): the base derives its `frame_cycles` by the rate law (L6), its schedule's iterations, or with no schedule its placed ports' most beats a frame, at its `interval` (L6's 1, or its own: Pool's, assumed), each port carrying its kernel's interval with its pace (`timed_pace`), which FIFO sizing reads; a kernel's `assumed` names what its cycles rest on that is assumed; composed kernels collect their actual members' answers with `Members`. Physical module ownership, required declarations and resources are checked when the class is made. A kernel with no cycles to count (no placed port, no member stating them) is refused (`kernel-cycles`). |
| `laws.py` | the library's interaction laws (L1-L10: handshake, pacing (a port's own schedule, or one of three ways to follow another port), rate, reset, clock, markers, idle and packing), stated once and inherited, a kernel stating only its exceptions; and the defaults they fix, which the base, the ports and the transports read here: each transport's pin names (`Axis`, `Native`: a port declares its transport kind, not its pins, and its kernel names a pin only where its RTL differs), the two interface protocols (`AXIS_PROTOCOL`, `NATIVE_PROTOCOL`: a kernel's `interface_protocol`, which also gives the default transport of a port of its body) and the doubled clock (`doubled`); the rate law's one exception, a kernel's initiation interval (`Interval`, L6's `RATE_INTERVAL` unless stated with its `Standing`: structural, measured or assumed), the one conversion from iterations to cycles (`in_cycles`) and a port's pace in cycles (`TimedPace`). Ports whose names collide are refused by name (`port-pins-collide`) |
| `port_model.py`, `port.py` | real typed child ports: one construct, `StreamPort`, declared as `InPort` or `OutPort` (its kind is its direction, `direction` a read-only derived from which its bus role follows, `stream_endpoint`; a `StreamPort` of neither is refused, `port-direction`, and so is a port class declaring its own `direction` (`port-direction`) or its `pacing` or `follows` (`port-pacing`); its tensor, element and transport kind, which defaults to its kernel's interface protocol's where it states none), exactly one presentation, a given sequence or its kernel's schedule's projection (an input with an index binds its kernel's extents), and its relation (`governs` and `dtype` an input's, `sourced` an output's; a selector's `group`), or idle; the base refuses a port's declaration when its kernel's class is made (`port-direction`, `port-presentation`, `port-pacing-ambiguous`, `port-interval`, `port-schedule-unstated`, `port-schedule-inapplicable`), and a placed port's own constraint (`presented`) refuses a design point whose port presents nothing, or a stream its transport cannot carry; what paces its beats follows from its declaration (`pacing`: its kernel's `SCHEDULE`, which it owns, or it `FOLLOWS` another port, and `follows` says how: `PASS_THROUGH`, a stage's side, `GROUPS`, a selector, or `READER`, a memory's output); an intake naming a port that is no placed input of its kernel is refused (`intake-port-unknown`, the intake's refusal, not its design point's), and a stated intake's own refusal is reported under `stated_intake`; its label its kernel's (supplied by the base for a port of its body). Intrinsic rules stay on the child; folding and composition stay on the parent. A kernel with children exposes a member's port as its own by a node alias (`x = compute.x`: one node, its member's, read by two names; the selected case's for a selector), presented under the alias's name (`exposed_name`, `exposed_side`, `exposed_pins`: label `x`, its pins on the leaf the alias reaches, `PortPins.instance`); a port's nodes are all inputs or all outputs, by their kind (`port-direction`), and a port is exposed under one name (`port-exposed-twice`). The pace of a port that owns its pacing is its own (`pace`, over its kernel's one `schedule`, which every such port of a kernel's body presents: `port-schedule-foreign`), and so is its interval (`interval`, its kernel's, carried with its pace: `timed_pace`; a port of a kernel's body states none, `port-interval`); `stream_port` and `port_direction` find a port by name, and `stream_ports` lists a kernel's own, `exposed_ports` its own and its exposed ones. Each port's `pin_values`, `held_values` and `pins_presented` read model inputs and call the pure pins/held helpers, for the base's module assembly; `OtherPins` declares a module's pins outside its ports and control bus. |
| `library.py` | plain `ModuleSpec` and resolved module/source/ABI assembly. A native kernel states parameters, sources and data; the base assembles its netlist. Constant sources are checked at definition; dynamic source provision is checked before data and held assembly. Control pins precede stream pins; stream holds precede control holds. A raised non-value halts evaluation; directly returned physical refusals accumulate and deduplicate after the calls finish. `library_module` reads the actual Leaf from the accepted netlist. |
| `channels.py`, `adapters.py` | one composed `Channel` per edge, its two sides (`sides.py`: a port's an input or an output side by its kind, the one role check the channel's, `channel-users`), its transport and finite adapter choices, source/mover placement, stage contracts, netlists, cycles (its members' but its transport's, each read where it is placed), buffering and resources. Its source's set port is exposed as the channel's (`set = source.set`); selected sources alone are demanded. Wires contribute no stage cycles. |
| `dotp.py`, `matmul.py`, `thresholding.py`, `eltwise.py`, `fifo.py`, `memstream.py`, `input_generator.py`, `transpose.py`, `vpc.py`, `pool.py`, `pwpolyf.py` | the kernels (`pool.py`: the first HLS kernel, over `pooling_stream`; `pwpolyf.py`: GELU, SiLU, Sigmoid or Tanh by piecewise polynomials on DSP58, FLOAT32 and approximate) |
| `configure.py`, `policies.py` | committing choices by key, and naming the open and the stored ones; each choice's policy (fixed, first valid in listed order or in a ranking view's, or required), stated by the compiler: a KernelOp for its kernel's choices, the channel for its own (`Channel.policies`, in `channels.py`), read through one reader (`policy`) and applied once (`defaulted`; a partition's in one pass over its design space in its flow order, each choice's policy read from its member's owner, `CoreComposition.defaulted`): neither the engine nor a kernel states one |
| `composer.py` | the composer: a core declared (`DataflowCoreDeclaration`, with its name, its order, each kernel's owner and a slice's frozen neighbours) by its kernels (each its class and facts, `Facts`) and its channels (each by tensor, its sides as a kernel's `Port` or a `Side` given as a value, its facts values or `Stated` by a member), made one `composite()` Space class per structure: a Param per fact at the root (`<member>-<fact>`), the kernels' formals bound to them only, each channel's sides to its kernels' sides below their member's node, a side's role the channel's to check, not the composer's; names refused by name (`name-shared`, `name-invalid`, `name-reserved`, `shell` among them); the class's names and owners its facts (`DataflowCoreKernel.names`, `policy_owners`); classes kept by structure, whether in a shell, and the engine's test modes in a bounded cache under a lock (`CLASS_CACHE_BOUND`). `compose` returns its design space, nothing chosen. It never reads a graph: the graph layer declares a core (`finn.custom_op.kernels.network.declaration`) |
| `budgets.py` | the shell's budgets as rules of a partition in its shell: its `shell` member (`ShellBudgets`), the row's facts and a Param per contributor bound to its member's derived value (a kernel's presented bus, `memory_initiators` and `takes_doubled_clock`, a boundary channel's movers, a valued channel's doubled clock), its rules (`within_control_budget`, `within_memory_ports`, one clock rule per contributor) and its counts (`counts`), which emission checks the netlist's against |
| `dataflow_core.py` | the core: the composer's classes' base (`DataflowCoreKernel`: its edge ports, its netlist's edge, its module named `finn_dataflowcore_<digest>`, its name left out of the digest, a slice's frozen neighbours read as its own, `slice-not-emitted`; a placed outer port no edge port names refused by its edge, `port-unconnected`), the keys mapped between a path and FINN's `(owner, key)` (`Names`), and a core's stored choices bound with the engine's `bind` (`bound`, `Bound`: a value outside its domain dropped as stale, with why). The graph layer declares and binds a graph's (`finn.custom_op.kernels.network`), a test one by hand (kernels by name as `Facts`, channels by tensor, each side a `Port` or a `Side`) |
| `explore.py`, `fifo_sizing.py` | the DSE seam (`Seam`) over a composition (`CoreComposition`: one design space of a core, a node's, a slice's or one declared by hand, read by `(owner, key)`), its keys `(owner, key)` pairs (`dotted` joins one), by member in rank order, each member's in its flow order: its one change (`set`, one engine change, refused with each rule it breaks and the choices standing in its way; by the fold rule, a change refused where stored channel choices stand in its way made again with them cleared, then each channel choice it cleared or brought into play set by the channel's policy, the strategy's), the open choices with their possible values (`choices`), its strategies (`Ranked`, `Hold`, `TargetThroughput`, `MaxThroughput`, `SizeFifos`, `Defaults`, the debug `DebugPlaceholder`), and a strategy's run (`reopen` its scope, search, `restore` what it reopened, a value that cannot be restored set by its policy); a channel's FIFO depth from both sides' beat patterns |
| `control.py`, `movers.py`, `utilization.py` | control buses (`ControlModel`, its included bus model, held idle or presented, declared once, its `Control` the kernel's member `bus_control`, which the base derives with `declares_bus`; a partition presents its members'), a boundary's mover (`IodmaMover`: what a shell offers at a free side, its contract and resources), and the kernels' cost model: a memory's primitives on the platform's fabric, its styles, the fitted counts (`utilization.py`, over `finn.platform.capabilities`' `Fabric` and `Resources`; `uram_constraint`, `uram_init_constraint`) |
| `values/` | the values kernels hold and ports admit: the semantics of datatypes, integer vectors and tensors, and threshold tables; the `Integer` port policy |
| `transport.py` | ready/valid and AXI-Stream transports (`ReadyValidTransport`, `AxisBeat`), and a port's stream on its pins, what a channel wires to (`StreamPins`: the port's `Stream` and which pin carries each marker; no label, which is the port's, `base.PortPins`, and no pace, which a port that owns its pacing has) |
| `artifacts/` | module values (`Leaf`, a flat `Composed` netlist) and their ABI (`abi.py`, with a module's clocking pins, `Clocking`), the top (`top.py`: a netlist wrapped as a `Composed` with FINN's top conventions, `compose`), their sources (`contributions.py`: copied files, generated data, HLS build requests), their emission, an HLS request's staging (`hls.py`), the RTL checker (and `evaluate`, the constants a module derives at elaboration), the IP-XACT packaging Tcl (`ipxact.py`), a packaged module's `interface.json` (`interface.py`) and the canonical digest (`projection.py`) |

FinnLib is the `finnlib` resource (`src/finn/resources.toml`); set
`FINN_RESOURCES_FINNLIB` to work against a clone. Checks:
`bash scripts/check-kernels.sh`.
