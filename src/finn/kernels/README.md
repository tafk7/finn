# `finn.kernels`

Physical kernels: each binds one FinnLib RTL module (or an HLS top FINN writes over a
FinnLib HLS component) or places kernel children, never both,
in a design space in `finn.core.space`, with the order it presents on each port.
The package never reads an ONNX graph; the KernelOps, which do, live in
`finn.custom_op.kernels`. Experimental: its design and authoring guide are maintained
outside this repository until the package is final.

| Module | Holds |
|---|---|
| `base.py` | the one kernel base, `Kernel`; a reader types a design point `Kernel` and reads its four answers. A channel's FIFO answers its cycles inapplicable, which its parent's `member_cycles` leaves out. A kernel binding a module states its binding (`module_spec`, `parameters`, `module_sources`, `module_data`) and its ports; the base assembles its netlist from them, reading its other pins, control bus and ports serially. Native kernels state their cycle calculations; composed kernels collect their actual members' answers with `Members`. Physical module ownership, required declarations and resources are checked when the class is made. A missing cycle calculation is refused. |
| `laws.py` | the library's interaction laws (L1-L10: handshake, advance per rule, rate, reset, clock, markers, idle and packing), stated once and inherited, a kernel stating only its exceptions; and the defaults they fix, which the base, the ports and the transports read here: each transport's pin names (`Axis`, `Native`: a port declares its transport kind, not its pins, and its kernel names a pin only where its RTL differs), the two clock domains (`AXIS_CLOCKING`, `NATIVE_CLOCKING`) and the doubled clock (`doubled`). Ports whose names collide are refused by name (`port-pins-collide`) |
| `port_model.py`, `port.py` | real typed child ports: one construct, `StreamPort`, presenting a given sequence or its kernel's schedule's projection (a target with an index binds its kernel's extents), or idle; its advance rule follows from its declaration (`advance`); its transport kind stated, its label and clock its kernel's (supplied by the base for a port of its body). Intrinsic rules stay on the child; folding and composition stay on the parent. A kernel with children serves a child's port as its own (`served`; the selected case's for a selector, labelled `compute.<case>.x`), which the base lowers into its side and contract (`x_side`, `x_contract`); `port_side`, `port_contract` and `port_endpoint` find any port by name through lexical Groups, stopping at real Spaces, and `stream_ports` lists a kernel's own. Each port's `pin_values`, `held_values` and `pins_presented` read model inputs and call the pure pins/held helpers, for the base's module assembly; `OtherPins` declares a module's pins outside its ports and control bus. |
| `library.py` | plain `ModuleSpec` and resolved module/source/ABI assembly. A native kernel states parameters, sources and data; the base assembles its netlist. Constant sources are checked at definition; dynamic source provision is checked before data and held assembly. Control pins precede stream pins; stream holds precede control holds. A raised non-value halts evaluation; directly returned physical refusals accumulate and deduplicate after the calls finish. `library_module` reads the actual Leaf from the accepted netlist. |
| `channels.py`, `adapters.py` | one composed `Channel` per edge, its transport and finite adapter choices, source/mover placement, stage contracts, netlists, cycles, buffering and resources. Its source's set port is served as the channel's (`set`); selected sources alone are demanded. Wires contribute no stage cycles. |
| `dotp.py`, `matmul.py`, `thresholding.py`, `eltwise.py`, `fifo.py`, `memstream.py`, `input_generator.py`, `transpose.py`, `vpc.py`, `pool.py`, `pwpolyf.py` | the kernels (`pool.py`: the first HLS kernel, over `pooling_stream`; `pwpolyf.py`: GELU, SiLU, Sigmoid or Tanh by piecewise polynomials on DSP58, FLOAT32 and approximate) |
| `configure.py`, `policies.py` | committing choices by key, and naming the open and the stored ones; each choice's policy (fixed, first valid in listed order or in a ranking view's, or required), stated by the compiler: a KernelOp for its kernel's choices, the channel for its own (`Channel.policies`, in `channels.py`), read through one reader (`policy`) and applied once (`defaulted`; a partition's in one pass over its design space in its flow order, each choice's policy read from its member's owner, `Partition.defaulted`): neither the engine nor a kernel states one |
| `composer.py` | the composer: a partition declared by its kernels (each its class and facts, `Facts`) and its channels (each by tensor, its sides as a kernel's `Port` or a `Side` given as a value, its facts values or `Stated` by a member), made one `composite()` Space class per structure: a Param per fact at the root (`<member>-<fact>`), the kernels' formals bound to them only, each channel's sides to its kernels' sides below their member's node; the side structural, from the ports' declarations (`channel-users`); names refused by name (`name-shared`, `name-invalid`, `name-reserved`, `shell` among them); keys mapped between a path and FINN's `(owner, key)` (`Names`); classes kept by structure, whether in a shell, and the engine's test modes in a bounded cache under a lock (`CLASS_CACHE_BOUND`). It never reads a graph: the graph layer declares a partition (`finn.custom_op.kernels.network.declaration`) |
| `budgets.py` | the shell's budgets as rules of a partition in its shell: its `shell` member (`ShellBudgets`), the row's facts and a Param per contributor bound to its member's derived value (a kernel's presented bus, `memory_initiators` and `takes_doubled_clock`, a boundary channel's movers, a valued channel's doubled clock), its rules (`within_control_budget`, `within_memory_ports`, one clock rule per contributor) and its counts (`counts`), which emission checks the netlist's against |
| `partition.py` | the partition: one design space of the composer's class (`Partition`), and what FINN reads of it by `(owner, key)` (its members ranked, each member's answers relative to it, every choice's state, the possible values of those asked, a change as one `try_with_choices`); a declaration composed and its stored choices bound with the engine's `bind` (`partition`: a value outside its domain dropped as stale, a rule they break binding, read refused); its views read out of the design space (the netlist with each channel's outer hops from the kernels' contract views, FIFO sizing's paces, its members' resources), its module (`artifacts/top.py`'s `compose`). The graph layer declares and binds a graph's (`finn.custom_op.kernels.network`), a test one by hand (kernels by name as `Facts`, channels by tensor, each side a `Port` or a `Side`) |
| `explore.py`, `fifo_sizing.py` | the DSE seam (`Seam`) over a composition (`Composition`: a partition's design space, a graph's or one declared by hand), its keys `(owner, key)` pairs (`dotted` joins one), by member in rank order, each member's in its flow order: its one change (`set`, one engine change, refused with each rule it breaks and the choices standing in its way; by the fold rule, a change refused where stored channel choices stand in its way made again with them cleared, then each channel choice it cleared or brought into play set by the channel's policy, the strategy's), the open choices with their possible values (`choices`), its strategies (`Ranked`, `Hold`, `TargetThroughput`, `MaxThroughput`, `SizeFifos`, `Defaults`, the debug `DebugPlaceholder`), and a strategy's run (`reopen` its scope, search, `restore` what it reopened, a value that cannot be restored set by its policy); a channel's FIFO depth from both sides' beat patterns |
| `control.py`, `movers.py`, `utilization.py` | control buses (`ControlModel`, its included bus model, held idle or presented, its `Control` the kernel's member `bus_control`; a partition presents its members'), a boundary's mover (`IodmaMover`: what a shell offers at a free side, its contract and resources), and the kernels' cost model: a memory's primitives on the platform's fabric, its styles, the fitted counts (`utilization.py`, over `finn.platform.capabilities`' `Fabric` and `Resources`; `uram_constraint`, `uram_init_constraint`) |
| `values/` | the values kernels hold and ports admit: the semantics of datatypes, integer vectors and tensors, and threshold tables; the `Integer` port policy |
| `transport.py` | ready/valid and AXI-Stream transports (`ReadyValidTransport`, `AxisBeat`), and the contract of one side of a channel (`StreamContract`, with its `pace`) |
| `artifacts/` | module values (`Leaf`, a flat `Composed` netlist) and their ABI (`abi.py`, with a module's clocking pins, `Clocking`), the top (`top.py`: a netlist wrapped as a `Composed` with FINN's top conventions, `compose`), their sources (`contributions.py`: copied files, generated data, HLS build requests), their emission, an HLS request's staging (`hls.py`), the RTL checker (and `evaluate`, the constants a module derives at elaboration), the IP-XACT packaging Tcl (`ipxact.py`), a packaged module's `interface.json` (`interface.py`) and the canonical digest (`projection.py`) |

FinnLib is the `finnlib` resource (`src/finn/resources.toml`); set
`FINN_RESOURCES_FINNLIB` to work against a clone. Checks:
`bash scripts/check-kernels.sh`.
